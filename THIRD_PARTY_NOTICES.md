## Service names and icons

Service icons in `Sources/Stash/Resources/ServiceIcons` identify link destinations
or source applications. Figma and Google document marks are drawn in SwiftUI.
Source URLs and acquisition details are recorded in [the asset inventory](Assets/ServiceIcons/README.md).

The project maintainer confirmed permission to redistribute the existing service
logos on September 28, 2026. These third-party assets retain their owners' rights;
they are **not relicensed under Stash's GPL grant**. This repository does not grant
trademark rights or imply endorsement by any service shown. Keep this notice and
the asset inventory with redistributions, and obtain applicable permission for
uses outside the authorized scope.

Recognition is offline. Clipboard content is not transmitted to these services to
retrieve icons or labels. Source-app icons are resolved from installed apps locally.

Swift, SwiftUI, AppKit, CryptoKit, and ImageIO are supplied by the Apple toolchain
or operating system. No third-party runtime library is embedded by the Swift package.
